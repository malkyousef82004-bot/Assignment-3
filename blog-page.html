The Power of Sports: Building Body and Mind

By Malak Abu Moghaisib | March 26, 2026

Engaging in regular physical activity is one of the most vital habits for maintaining a healthy lifestyle. Sports not only strengthen the muscles and heart, but they also improve focus, discipline, and emotional well-being.

Health & Mental Benefits of Exercise

Regular workouts boost cardiovascular health, reduce daily stress, and increase energy levels throughout the day. Whether it's running, swimming, or team sports, staying active plays a key role in preventing chronic diseases.
